<h2>Understanding the Sedge Evolution and Conservation in Southeast Asia</h2>
<h4>Ayuda A1 - Plan Propio Investigación PPI2606 (PI: José Ignacio Márquez-Corro, 5,700€)</h4>

The activities focus on reducing the project’s workload and enhancing the perceived feasibility of the proposal by organizing tasks into more compact work packages:

1. Photodocumentation of taxonomically diagnostic organs.
The taxonomy of Cyperaceae (sedges) relies on the micromorphology of their reproductive structures. Accurately delimiting species often requires detailed observation of glumes, perigynia, and nutlets. The PI will carry out high-quality photodocumentation of these organs, using the stereomicroscopy and image-stacking equipment. This activity will accelerate the taxonomic review of herbarium material and new collections and will also generate a curated image database to be integrated into the Global Cyperaceae Database (cyperaceae.org). This visual resource will reduce taxonomic uncertainty, facilitate identification by local collaborators, and provide the morphological evidence needed to support future proposals for new species in the upcoming application to the *Plan Estatal*.

2. DNA extraction and phylogenetic analysis using genomic sequences for *Carex* lineages.
The genus *Carex* is one of the most diverse and taxonomically complex lineages within the family. Resolving its evolutionary relationships represents a bottleneck for the project. The PI will perform the bioinformatic processing of genomic data obtained via the low-copy-number gene capture technique (HybSeq), using a kit specifically designed for Cyperaceae. Tasks will span from laboratory work to sequence assembly, alignment, and the execution of phylogenetic pipelines. This work will yield a robust phylogenomic framework for the *Carex* lineages present in Indochina — an essential requirement for correctly interpreting patterns of diversification and endemism, and for conducting accurate conservation assessments.
